A request for n8n integrations from SequentDraw's own catalogue is answered
from the catalogue (`sequentdraw catalogue`, offered by business-map and
eval-build), not by searching GitHub. The resemblance is what could
mis-route this: both answer "what software would help here". The difference
is the source -- a fixed catalogue of hosted integrations against an
open-ended search of open-source repositories -- and gitrepo-suggest must
not fire for the first.
